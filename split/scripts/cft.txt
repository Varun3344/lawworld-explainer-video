# 2 · THE CASE
10 | This court fee, under Section Seventy Six, feeds the Legal Benefit Fund. A notification raised it to one percent, in force from first April, twenty sixteen. || This court fee, under Section 76, feeds the Legal Benefit Fund. A notification raised it to 1%, in force from 1 April 2016.
10 | Twelve tax assessees, among them Sobha and Manappuram Jewellers, filed appeals and revisions after the notification, and were told to pay the new rate.
# 3 · THE CLASH
10 | Their plea: the hike impairs a vested right of appeal, and cannot reach older assessment years. The State said every appeal filed after the notification pays.
# 4 · WHEN THE RIGHT VESTS
10 | The Court held: the right of appeal, with its court fee, vests when the lis begins. That is Hoosein Kasam Dada, approved by a Constitution Bench, which still binds.
10 | Nothing in the notification makes it retrospective. So the one percent fee applies only where the lis began after first April, twenty sixteen. || Nothing in the notification makes it retrospective. So the 1% fee applies only where the lis began after 1 April 2016.
# 5 · WHEN A TAX LIS BEGINS
10 | A tax lis begins when the officer, not satisfied with the return, calls for evidence. Sobha's notice came in twenty fifteen, so it need not pay the enhanced fee. || A tax lis begins when the officer, not satisfied with the return, calls for evidence. Sobha's notice came in 2015, so it need not pay the enhanced fee.
# 6 · THE RESULT
10 | Manappuram's books were called for only in twenty eighteen, so it pays the new rate. In five petitions with no dates pleaded, the appellate forum must find them. || Manappuram's books were called for only in 2018, so it pays the new rate.
