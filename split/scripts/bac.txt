// "freezing order" issued by the 2nd respondent police officer (1). He explains the disputed entries as sums given to him by his cousin
// and her husband; alternatively, confine any freeze to the disputed amount alone (2, his case). The Single Judge dismissed the writ
// petition, "but then moved a step forward", directing that his account itself be investigated by the police (3); after finding against

// account of the petitioner" (8). His counsel: he explained the source of the money; no other investigation pending against him,

// counsel: the bank has received no information from R2 or R3 of any investigation launched, initiated or pending against him or his
