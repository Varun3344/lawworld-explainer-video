// Held: size is relevant; a large company has a different FAR profile; TNMM is tolerant of variations but does not accommodate an
// extremely large disparity in size; size may significantly alter the cost structure (50); an established brand and goodwill are
// intangible assets with a vital bearing on revenue margins; brand is not the only variable, but bears on profit margins (64, 69);
// functional dissimilarity is a finding of fact, not perverse (22, 25, 37, 40, 46, 53); a segment can be compared only with credible
// segmental financials; s.133(6) information must be credible and duly verified; a question of fact case by case (57, 58); the ITAT
// may rely on another assessee's order if the material facts are similar (60); narrowing the RPT filter is appropriate if sufficient
// comparables are found, and here they were (61-63); Q5 and Q8 answered against the Revenue (63, 71); in the given facts the questions
// are not substantial questions of law (72); appeal dismissed (73).

# 1 · GIANTS AS COMPARABLES
10 | Hi, I am Ravishankar from LawWorld. Your client writes software for its own group. Can the transfer pricing officer compare it with giants like Infosys and Wipro?
10 | On twenty eighth October, a Karnataka High Court bench of Honourable Chief Justice Vibhu Bakhru and Honourable Justice K S Hemalekha ruled. || On 28 October, a Karnataka High Court bench of Honourable Chief Justice Vibhu Bakhru and Honourable Justice K.S. Hemalekha ruled.
# 2 · THE ADJUSTMENT
10 | Logica, a software services company, faced a transfer pricing adjustment of nearly forty crore rupees, for assessment year two thousand seven to eight. || Logica, a software services company, faced a transfer pricing adjustment of nearly ₹40 crore, for assessment year 2007-08.
10 | The Transfer Pricing Officer picked twenty six comparables, including Infosys and Wipro. Their average markup on cost was about twenty five percent. || The Transfer Pricing Officer picked 26 comparables, including Infosys and Wipro. Their average markup on cost was about 25%.
10 | The Tribunal struck out eight comparables, Infosys and Wipro among them, and tightened the related party filter from twenty five to fifteen percent. || The Tribunal struck out eight comparables, Infosys and Wipro among them, and tightened the related party filter from 25% to 15%.
# 3 · THE REVENUE'S APPEAL
10 | The Revenue appealed. After a Supreme Court remand, it argued that brand and size don't affect margins, and that the fifteen percent filter had no basis. || The Revenue appealed. After a Supreme Court remand, it argued that brand and size don't affect margins, and that the 15% filter had no basis.
# 4 · WHAT THE COURT HELD
10 | The High Court disagreed. Size matters: a giant's cost structure may differ. T N M M tolerates variations, but not an extremely large gap in size. || The High Court disagreed. Size matters: a giant's cost structure may differ. TNMM tolerates variations, but not an extremely large gap in size.
10 | An established brand bears on margins, so Infosys and Wipro were rightly excluded. Product, training and drug discovery firms were found functionally different.
# 5 · THE VERDICT
10 | The fifteen percent related party filter was upheld, as enough comparables remained. No substantial question of law arose. The appeal was dismissed. || The 15% related party filter was upheld, as enough comparables remained. No substantial question of law arose. The appeal was dismissed.
