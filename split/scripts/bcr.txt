# 1 · DEVELOPER TURNED BUYER?
10 | Hi, I am Ravishankar from LawWorld. Your client was the developer, the joint venture ended, and it agreed to buy a flat instead. Can it still go to RERA?
10 | On the thirtieth of October, twenty twenty six, a single bench of Honourable Justice P B Balaji of the Madras High Court answered that question. || On 30 October 2026, a single bench of Honourable Justice P.B. Balaji of the Madras High Court answered that question.
# 2 · THE ROLE REVERSAL
10 | In twenty twelve, Bennett Property Holdings signed a joint venture agreement with land owners to develop their land. Later, by consent, it was terminated. || In 2012, Bennett Property Holdings signed a joint venture agreement with land owners to develop their land. Later, by consent, it was terminated.
10 | Then the roles reversed. The land owners would build, and Bennett would buy an apartment, through an agreement of sale and a separate construction agreement.
10 | Bennett says the land owners, now the builders, delayed construction and did not hand over possession. So it sought compensation before the Tamil Nadu RERA.
# 3 · REJECTED AS COMMERCIAL
10 | In July, citing the old joint venture, the Adjudicating Officer rejected it as a monetary deal, triable only by a Commercial Court.
# 4 · WHAT THE COURT HELD
10 | The High Court held that Bennett is an allottee under Section Two of RERA. Buying its flat with an undivided share is not a commercial transaction. || The High Court held that Bennett is an allottee under Section 2 of RERA. Buying its flat with an undivided share is not a commercial transaction.
10 | Once the joint venture ended, the parties became strangers, so that old deal cannot be used against the buyer. Land owners who turn developers are bound by RERA.
# 5 · THE RESULT
10 | Calling the officer's finding perverse, the Court allowed the revision. If in order, the complaint must be numbered, the land owners heard, and decided on merits.
