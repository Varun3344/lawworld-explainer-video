# 5 · WHAT THE COURT HELD
10 | Finally, what did the High Court decide? Nothing in the advertisement required the father's name rather than the husband's. That was no ground for the general list.
10 | The issuing officer ranks above a Circle Officer, and her caste was not disputed. Such technicalities could not defeat her candidature; the appeal failed.
