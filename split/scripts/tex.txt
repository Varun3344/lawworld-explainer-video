// Facts used: the Textiles Committee, a statutory body under s.3 of the Textiles Committee Act, 1963 (3); s.5A (inserted by Act 51 of
// 1973 w.e.f. 01.01.1975): a cess, as a duty of excise, on all textiles and textile machinery manufactured in India, not exceeding 1% ad
// valorem; proviso: no cess on textiles manufactured from out of handloom or powerloom industry (5, 6.4); 'manufacture' not defined (5.1);
// powerloom industry: not more than 50 powerlooms (s.2(db), 5.3); the company bleaches, dyes and processes 100% man-made fabrics supplied
// by powerloom and handloom units, on job work (3, 3.2, 6.3); show cause notice 20.08.2001 and assessment order 21.11.2001: textile cess
// Rs 2,68,293 (3.2, 3.3); the Appellate Tribunal (T.C.A. No. 223 of 2002), relying on Chandok Textile Exporters: processing amounted to
// manufacture, appeal dismissed (2, 3.4 - its date is given two ways, so no date is used); High Court at Allahabad, 06.03.2013, Writ Tax
// No. 354 of 2006: bleaching and dyeing by job workers does not obliterate the status of the textile as a powerloom product, exempt under
// s.5A; job workers "an extended hand" of the manufacturers (1, 3.5-3.5.2); the Committee: adopt 'manufacture' from s.2(f) of the Central
// Excise Act, 1944 (any process incidental or ancillary to the completion of a manufactured product) (5.4, 6).
// Held: unless brought in by reference or incorporation, a word's definition in another statute cannot be applied (5.5, 5.6; R.C. Jain,
// (1981) 2 SCC 308); every statute has its own context, scheme and purpose (5.6); Jagatram Ahuja (2000) 8 SCC 249, Maheshwari Fish Seed Farm
// (2004) 4 SCC 705, G.P. Singh (5.7-5.8.2); 'manufactured' in s.5A "has to be understood in its common parlance and not in any special
// sense by borrowing" (6.1); the company receives, dyes, bleaches and returns the textile: no manufacturing; "an extended arm" of the
// powerloom manufacturers (6.3, 6.5, 6.6); "At the best, it is the textile upgraded"; not liable to textile cess (6.7); appeal dismissed (7).
// Flow policy: a tax case, no accusation; the company's name stays in a neutral sentence.

# 4 · WHAT THE COURT HELD
10 | The Supreme Court disagreed. A word undefined in one Act cannot take another Act's definition, unless brought in by reference or incorporation.
10 | Every statute has its own context. So manufactured in Section Five A takes its common parlance meaning, not a sense borrowed from another statute. || Every statute has its own context. So "manufactured" in Section 5A takes its common parlance meaning, not a sense borrowed from another statute.
# 5 · THE VERDICT
10 | The company's dyeing and bleaching on job work was not manufacture: at best, the textile was upgraded. So no cess was due, and the appeal was dismissed.
