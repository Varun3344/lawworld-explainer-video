# 5 · WHAT THE COURT HELD
10 | Finally, what did the High Court decide? The family list decides whose own accident is insured. But the death benefit goes to the nominee or legal heir.
10 | The insurer applied the wrong clause. The unmarried sister is entitled as legal heir. The rejection was quashed, and the insurer must pay within four weeks.
