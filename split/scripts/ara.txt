// Facts used: the 4th respondent, a housing cooperative society (Peenya, Bangalore), signed the Annexure P1 agreement with a third
// party (Bhagya Enterprises) on 21.09.1984 - the third party would ensure acquisition "as government land" for the society (37-38);
// s.3(f) with sub-clause (vi) substituted by Act 68 of 1984 w.e.f. 24.09.1984 (3, 37, 39); Government approval letter 13.11.1987, 97
// acres (13, 45); s.4(1) notification 22.11.1988 (79 acres 25 guntas), s.6 declaration 04.12.1989 (59 acres 6 guntas) (12-13); landowners
// went to the High Court in 2007 when interference with their possession was attempted, WP 9412 of 2007 (12); the Single Judge quashed the acquisition for
// fraud and colourable exercise of power, found possession never taken (no mahazar), costs Rs 10,000 each (15-16); the Division Bench
// reversed on delay and res judicata (Karithimmaiah, 2002 (1) Kar.L.J. 469 (DB)) (17-20).
// Held: s.3(f)(vi) approval "is not of the mere requisition made, by a Cooperative Society. It should be for the scheme ... forma et
// descriptio (the layout and the blueprint)" (40, 43); no scheme or layout was placed before the Government (40, 43, 71(v)); the 1984
// agreement, before such schemes were a public purpose, "clearly exposes the fraud" (71(i)); "Fraud vitiates all solemn acts" (56);
// res judicata and delay "pale into insignificance" (72); SLP dismissal in Karithimmaiah is no precedent under Art. 141 (25, 66);
// DB set aside, Single Judge restored, contempt direction deleted, costs enhanced to Rs 50,000 each (73); possession restored to all
// landowners (challengers or not) on refund of the Government compensation without interest; the society has no claim; plots not
// resumed are auctioned, proceeds to victim compensation (74); appeals allowed (75).
// Flow policy: no private person's name in a spoken line; the society is never named; fraud is said only as the Court's finding.

# 4 · WHAT THE COURT HELD
10 | The Supreme Court disagreed. Under Section Three f six, the Government must approve the scheme itself, with its layout and blueprint, not the society's mere requisition. || The Supreme Court disagreed. Under Section 3(f)(vi), the Government must approve the scheme itself, with its layout and blueprint, not the society's mere requisition.
10 | Here, no layout was placed before the Government, only a sketch of the land. And the agreement, before such schemes were a public purpose, exposed the fraud.
10 | Fraud vitiates all solemn acts, the Court said, and res judicata and delay pale into insignificance. The Single Judge's order is restored. || "Fraud vitiates all solemn acts," the Court said, and res judicata and delay pale into insignificance. The Single Judge's order is restored.
