// Facts used (para): petition under Articles 226 / 227 r/w s.482 CrPC to set aside the complaint dated 19.05.2016 in C.C. No.
// 36296/2016 (Metropolitan Magistrate, Traffic Court-1, Mayo Hall, Bengaluru) and the order of 26.11.2016, so far as the petitioner
// is concerned (cause title, 2). The Inspector of Legal Metrology filed a private complaint alleging violation of s.18 of the Legal
// Metrology Act r/w Rule 18(2) of the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodity) Rules, 2011 (3.1). The petitioner is accused no. 3; she
// claims she was shown as a non-executive director of the company whose premises were visited (3.2). The flying squad visited the
// premises, found some violation with regard to the weighing scale, seized it and initiated action (3.3). Her plea: under s.49 no
// prosecution is permissible against a non-executive director (4); unless the company is made a party, prosecution of the directors

// suitable orders may be passed (6). The Court examined Form No. 32 filed before the Registrar of Companies, a mandatory requirement
// (7); it shows her as a non-executive director (8). s.49(1): where an offence is committed by a company, (a)(i) the person nominated
// under s.49(2) to be in charge of, and responsible to, the company for the conduct of its business, or (ii) where none is nominated,
// every person in charge of and responsible to the company at the time, and (b) the company; Explanation (b) defines "director" (9).
