// Facts used (para): appeal under s.13(1A) Commercial Courts Act r/w s.32(9) SFC Act against the judgment and decree of 06.07.2026
// of the XI Addl. District & Sessions Judge (Dedicated Commercial Court), Bengaluru Rural, in Commercial Misc. Petition 12/2020 filed
// by KSFC (1, p3). KSFC's recovery proceedings under ss.31(1)(aa) and 32 SFC Act against M/s. Prashanth Minerals Exports Pvt Ltd, the
// guarantor P.S. Raju and M/s. Karunai Granites Pvt Ltd for Rs.2,40,64,651 as on 10.03.2000 with future interest at 16.5% p.a.
// quarterly rests (2). The appellant is the legal heir of the deceased guarantor (3; cause title "S/O LATE P S RAJU"). Deeds of
// Guarantee 16.01.1989 and 17.12.1989, unconditional and irrevocable; liability continuing, joint and several, enforceable on default
// (3). KSFC term loans Rs.60,00,000 (sanction 20.07.1988) and Rs.13,50,000 (29.11.1989) to the company for a medium scale granite
// processing industry; equitable mortgage by deposit of title deeds besides the guarantees (4). Commercial Court, 06.07.2026: petition
// allowed with costs; all the respondents (including the appellant) jointly and severally liable for Rs.40,82,49,500.80 (claim with
// interest and charges from the petition to the order), costs and future interest at 16.5% compounded quarterly till realisation (5).
// Appellant: heirs liable only to the extent of the estate inherited; no personal liability merely as heirs (6). KSFC "does not
// dispute this legal position", but says the heirs have not furnished details of the properties inherited (7). Point: jointly and
// severally liable for the entire decretal amount, or only to the extent of the estate inherited? (8). Held: legal heirs do not
// ordinarily incur the guarantor's personal liability because they are heirs; recoverable from the inherited estate, not the heirs'
// independent assets, unless they separately assume the liability; SBI v. Jayanthi, AIR 2011 Mad 179 (s.131 Contract Act: death
// revokes the guarantee for future transactions, existing liability survives against the estate) (9); "The death of the guarantor does
// not extinguish guarantor's existing liability"; heirs inherit the estate subject to the liability; heirs' liability limited to the
// inherited estate, Ram Kishun v. State of U.P., (2012) 11 SCC 511 (10); s.128: surety coextensive with the principal debtor (11, 12);
// "The heirs' personal income and independently acquired assets should not be made liable merely by reason of inheritance";
// enforcement confined to the inherited properties, subject to the amount actually due and defences (12). ORDER: decree modified; the
// appellant and the other legal heirs liable only to the extent of the estate/properties inherited, not jointly and severally liable
// for the entire Rs.40,82,49,500.80 with interest and costs; the heirs to disclose to the Executing Court the properties inherited (13);
// appeal disposed of with that clarification and modification (14).

# 5 · WHAT THE COURT HELD
10 | Finally, what did the High Court decide? A guarantor's death does not end his existing liability. It continues against the estate the heirs inherit.
10 | But the heirs' own income and assets are not liable just by inheritance. The decree was modified: they answer only up to the estate, and must disclose it.
