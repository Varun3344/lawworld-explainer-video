// Facts used (para): the appeal questions the trial Court's order, upheld by the High Court, rejecting the plaint under Order VII
// Rule 11 CPC prior to registration of the civil suit (1). The PLAINTIFF'S CASE (2, 13; voiced only as "the plaintiff says"): owner of
// a piece of land settled on him by his son by a registered settlement deed of 26.02.2021; in need of money he borrowed Rs.25,00,000
// from the defendant's husband at interest of 24% per month; on the husband's insistence a sale deed of the land was registered on
// 15.03.2021 in the defendant's name; possession continued with him; the sale deed was only security for the loan; he offered to
// repay Rs.25,00,000 with interest; the defendant avoided executing a sale deed in his favour and attempted to take forcible
// possession on 25.05.2025. Reliefs: declaration of title + permanent injunction; redemption of "the mortgage deed, styled as sale
// deed dated 15.03.2021"; mandatory injunction to reconvey; costs (2). Trial court: presented before the Principal District Judge,
// Cuddalore; prior to registration the judge heard the plaintiff and perused the documents; the sale deed had no provision for
// re-sale and no agreement to that effect was filed, so the defendant had the full right of enjoyment; plaint rejected, refund of
// court fees (3); verbatim order: "The sale deed does not contain the resale provision. No agreement is filed by the plaintiff in mark
// of agreeing for resale." (12); Unnumbered O.S., order dated 11.07.2025 (19). High Court: a Division Bench of the Madras High Court
// held the rejection proper, no inference of a mortgage or conditional sale from the settlement deed or the sale deed; "for lack of
// cause of action, the plaint was rightly rejected"; suit not numbered, refund extended in appeal (4); A.S. No. 970 of 2025,
// 16.10.2025 (19). Plaintiff's counsel: Order VII Rule 11 cannot be used prior to registration, registration a ministerial act;
// alternatively the plaint discloses a cause of action (5, 10). Defendant's counsel: the registered sale deed was an outright sale,
// no inference of security; no bar to Rule 11 at any stage, even before registration (6).
// Held: the trial Court "erred in rejecting the plaint ... after entering into the merits of the case and without indicating how the
// plaint was liable to be rejected in a summary manner"; the High Court too erred; the suit warranted an adjudication on merits (7);
// Register of Civil Suits, Order IV Rule 2 (8); the plaintiff's contention that Rule 11 can be used only after registration "cannot be
// accepted" (10) - T. Arivandandam (1977 INSC 204), Saleem Bhai (2002 INSC 554), Madiraju Venkata Ramana Raju (2018 INSC 249), Church
// of Christ Charitable Trust (2012 INSC 264) (10.1-10.3); "In a given case, such power may be exercised even prior to registration of
// the plaint"; registration is no "shield of invincibility" (11); the trial Court named no clause of Rule 11 (12); "On a meaningful
// reading of the entire plaint, it is evident that the same discloses a cause of action"; not barred by law; the trial Court failed to
// notice the various prayers; not permissible to ignore material averments or refuse to read the plaint as a whole (14); the High
// Court did not consider the ground that the plaintiff had no chance to lead evidence (15); "Likelihood of a suit being dismissed
// after trial does not furnish a ground for the Court to reject the plaint prior to the trial. If a plaint discloses a cause of
// action, which it does in the present case, and the suit does not appear to be barred by any law ... it has to go for trial";
// Ganga Bai (1974 INSC 87); no ground under Order VII Rule 11 made out (16); rejection is a "decree" (s.2(2)), so a record of
// pre-registration rejections is needed, e.g. for appeals and Order VII Rule 13 (17); Selvaraj (Madras HC): "a roving enquiry into
// the merits of the matter was not called for" - cited as that HC decision's observation, not voiced (18). Orders: the trial Court's
// order of 11.07.2025 and the HC judgment of 16.10.2025 set aside; "The plaint is restored for being duly registered"; the suit to
// be decided in accordance with law; observations not an expression on the merits (19); whenever a trial Court rejects a plaint
// under Order VII Rule 11 prior to registration, it "shall be duly recorded in a register maintained for such purpose under Order IV
// Rule 2"; High Courts may act under Section 122 CPC (20); copy to the Registrar General of the High Courts and the Director of the
// State Judicial Academies (21); appeal allowed, no costs (22).
