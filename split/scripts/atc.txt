// Held: the Constitution "does not recognize any detention dehors arrest"; the 24 hours "would commence the moment a person is detained by
// the arresting authority suspecting him to have committed an offence, when his liberty is effectively curtailed" (58); the deadline runs
// "from the time when a person is intercepted and detained by the investigating agency ... when his liberty is curtailed" (64); facts:
// intercepted at 2 pm on 29.09.2025, liberty curtailed from then, under the search team's control; search completed 5 pm; shown formally
// arrested 30.09.2025 at 11:00 / 11:15 am; produced before the Judicial Magistrate 30.09.2025 at 4:45 pm (65); arrest for Article 22(2)
// at 2 pm on 29.09.2025, production due before 2 pm on 30.09.2025, a clear breach vitiating the arrest (66); an unexplained 18-hour gap
// (5 pm to 11 am), not a reasonable period under s.58 BNSS / s.57 CrPC (67). Conclusions 69(i)-(ix): (i) the 24 hours commence "from the
// date and time of detention of the person detained when his liberty stands curtailed and not from the time of his formal arrest";
// (ii) detention beyond 24 hours before production: release forthwith; (iii) only the journey time is excluded; (iv) Aryaman Pattnayak,
