// Facts used: petitioners are residents of fishing villages within 20 km north of Vizhinjam, where the seaport is being built; they
// CONTEND the construction (the breakwater) caused substantial erosion on the northern side and accretion on the southern side (1);
// Ext.P11 G.O. dated 06.10.2022: the State constituted an Expert Committee to examine whether there was coastal erosion consequent to
// the port work and to identify measures to address it, after hearing the local population (2); the petitioners' case: the members
// had a conflict of interest (1, 3, 6(ii), 14); the chairman an expert member of the "Technical Sub-committee" for the development of
// the Vizhinjam Seaport since 2012 (15(i)); another member President-Technical of a company which has a connection with the 7th
// respondent (concessionaire) (15(ii), 20, 6(vi)); the final report "diametrically opposite" to the chairman's 2010 paper (15(iv));
// the committee inspected Vizhinjam only once, 23.05.2023, met the inhabitants 24.05.2023; report approved by G.O. 02.08.2024 (23);
// the State's case: the erosion came from high wave activity and cyclones, "as has been reported by the Committee"; no fresh committee
// needed (7(ii), 7(iii)); delay and laches raised by the respondents (8(iii), 11).
// Held: delay and laches repelled, since the petition is with reference to the 2022 G.O. (11, 12); limited judicial review, no stay of
// the project sought (recorded 22.07.2026), so only the committee and its report are examined (13); the bias test: N.K. Bajpai (18),
// A.K. Kraipak: "a mere suspicion of bias is not sufficient. There must be a reasonable likelihood of bias" (19); the chairman's earlier
// involvement "not disputed by any of the respondents", so his inclusion "ought to have been avoided" (20); the other member's link
// "not seen denied" by respondents 6 and 7 (20); the challenge to a third member rejected: no challenge to his qualification (20); the
// report is "in stark contrast" to the chairman's own 2010 article, noted only given the uncontroverted conflict allegation (21); the
// challenge accepted (22), "all the more so" since only one inspection (23); the Government cannot now ignore its own committee's report
// because of the NGT committee (24); the Court declined to name members itself: for the Government to form a fresh committee (25).
// Orders (26): the committee's constitution under Ext.P11 declared flawed; its final report not to be acted upon; the Government to
// constitute an independent committee for the studies in Ext.P11, ensuring no conflict of interest. No time limit is fixed.
// Not said: the G.O. itself is NOT quashed (the new committee does the Ext.P11 studies); the Court made no finding on the erosion or on
// the report's merits (21); no finding of actual bias. Neutral on the port company and the Government: no company name in speech.
