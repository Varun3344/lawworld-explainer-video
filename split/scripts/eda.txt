// Kerala HC, Satish Motilal Bidri (2024 SCC OnLine Ker 3410): the only power against equivalent-value property with no link to the
// predicate offence is when the property was taken or held out of India; attachment of a 2004 property "ex-facie, ultra vires"
// (11.59, 11.60); it disagreed with the Delhi HC in Axis Bank (2019:DHC:1887) (11.61); Axis Bank (Delhi): other property of
// equivalent value may be attached where the tainted property is untraceable, cannot be reached or is deficient (11.72, 11.73).

# 1 · BOUGHT BEFORE THE CRIME
10 | Can the Enforcement Directorate attach property you bought years before the alleged crime even happened, so it was never bought with crime money?
10 | The Kerala High Court has said no, unless the proceeds went abroad. The Delhi High Court took a wider view. So which view would the Karnataka High Court take?

10 | Finally, what did the High Court decide? Property bought before the crime can still be attached for equal value, if the Act's conditions are met, even with nothing abroad.
