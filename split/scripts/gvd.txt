# 1 · WHAT MUST BE PROVED?
10 | When a person dies by suicide, what exactly must the prosecution prove in court, and how, before someone else can be convicted of abetting it?
10 | Here, a husband was sentenced to ten years, and the main evidence was four letters said to be in his handwriting. But did anyone actually prove he wrote them?

# 5 · WHAT THE COURT HELD
10 | Finally, what did the Supreme Court decide? The prosecution must stand on its own legs and prove abetment beyond reasonable doubt. A missing denial is not proof.
10 | The expert was never examined, so the letters were never proved. And even if he wrote them, they alone were not the immediate cause. So he was acquitted.
