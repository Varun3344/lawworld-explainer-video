# 1 · THE DECREE YOU NEVER SAW
8 | A money decree, in a suit you never knew about. Now your bank account is attached. Can that decree be undone?
8 | Hi, I'm Ravishankar from LawWorld. Today, Order Nine, Rule Thirteen of the C P C: setting aside an ex parte decree.
6 | An ex parte decree is passed in the defendant's absence, after the suit is heard without him.
# 2 · HOW A SUIT GOES EX PARTE
6 | Order Nine, Rule Six applies when the plaintiff appears but the defendant does not, at the hearing.
8 | If due service is proved, the court may order the suit to be heard ex parte. Otherwise, it must issue a second summons.
8 | Before the decree, Rule Seven lets him appear at or before the adjourned hearing, show good cause, and be heard on terms.
# 3 · A HYPOTHETICAL: SUNIL'S CASE
8 | A hypothetical case. Pallavi, a paper supplier, sues Sunil, a printer, for money due on unpaid invoices.
8 | The summons goes to a shop Sunil left two years ago. The process server reports that he affixed it to the door.
8 | The court hears the suit ex parte and passes a money decree. Sunil first learns of it when his bank account is attached.
# 4 · RULE 13: TWO GROUNDS
8 | After the decree, Rule Thirteen lets the defendant apply to the court that passed it, for an order setting it aside.
10 | The court shall set the decree aside if satisfied that the summons was not duly served, or that he was prevented by sufficient cause from appearing.
8 | It may impose terms as to costs, payment into court or otherwise, and then appoints a day for proceeding with the suit.
10 | Sunil relies on the first ground: under Order Five, Rule Seventeen, affixing is done where the defendant ordinarily resides or carries on business.
# 5 · THE PROVISOS AND THE EXPLANATION
8 | First proviso: a decree that cannot be set aside against one defendant only may be set aside against the others too.
10 | Second proviso: a mere irregularity in service is not enough, if the defendant had notice of the hearing date and sufficient time to appear.
6 | So if Sunil actually knew the date in time, irregular service alone will not help him.
10 | Under the Explanation, if an appeal against the ex parte decree was disposed of other than by withdrawal, no Rule Thirteen application lies.
# 6 · THE 30-DAY CLOCK
8 | Under Article One Twenty Three of the Limitation Act, you have thirty days from the date of the decree to apply.
8 | But where the summons was not duly served, the thirty days run instead from when the applicant had knowledge of the decree.
6 | If Sunil shows he was not duly served, his thirty days run from when he learnt of the decree.
8 | Missed it? Section Five of the Limitation Act lets the court admit a late application, on sufficient cause for the delay.
# 7 · WHAT COUNTS AS SUFFICIENT CAUSE
10 | On this second ground, in G P Srivastava versus R K Raizada, the Supreme Court held that sufficient cause must be liberally construed, to do complete justice. || On this second ground, in G.P. Srivastava versus R.K. Raizada, the Supreme Court held that sufficient cause must be liberally construed, to do complete justice.
10 | This applies particularly where no negligence or inaction is imputable to him. It is an elastic expression, and courts have wide discretion.
8 | The cause must relate to the date the court proceeded ex parte. Earlier lapses, already overlooked, cannot be held against him.
10 | If a defendant comes promptly, within time, and his absence was not mala fide or intentional, discretion is normally exercised in his favour.

6 | Costs can make up for any hardship caused, and the case is then decided on its merits.
# 8 · APPEAL, APPLICATION, OR BOTH
6 | Section Ninety Six, sub section Two, also allows an appeal from an ex parte decree.
8 | In Bhanu Kumar Jain versus Archana Kumar, the Supreme Court said a defendant may pursue both remedies simultaneously.
10 | But if the appeal is dismissed, the decree merges with the appellate order, and the Rule Thirteen application is no longer maintainable.
8 | The converse is not true. If the Rule Thirteen application fails, the appeal survives, but he cannot reargue sufficient cause there.
6 | In that appeal, he may argue that the evidence on record did not justify the decree.
# 9 · NOTICE, APPEAL AND EXECUTION
8 | Under Rule Fourteen, no decree is set aside unless notice of the application is served on the opposite party.
8 | If the Rule Thirteen application is rejected, an appeal lies under Order Forty Three, Rule One, clause D.
8 | An appeal alone is not a stay. Order Forty One, Rule Five lets the appellate court stay execution for sufficient cause.
# 10 · YOUR CHECKLIST
8 | So, act fast. Apply within thirty days of the decree, or of learning of it if the summons was not duly served.
8 | Get certified copies of the order sheet and the process server's report, and check the address and manner of service.
8 | Support sufficient cause with documents, offer costs, check whether an appeal was filed, and seek a stay of execution if needed.
