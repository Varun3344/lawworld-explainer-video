// Facts used (para): three petitions (W.P. 27611, 31717, 32771/2025) challenge Clause 33(ix) of the SLPRB notification Rc.No.161/
// SLPRB/Rect.2/2022 dated 28.11.2022; three (W.P. 29972/2025, 7016, 11918/2026) seek a declaration that not considering them for SCT
// Police Constable posts in the unfilled / left-over vacancies is illegal and arbitrary (1). G.O.Ms.No.153 of 20.10.2022: 6511 posts
// (SI, RSI, PC Civil, PC APSP); the Board's notification of 28.11.2022: 3580 PC (Civil) (men and women) + 2520 PC APSP (men) = 6100
// posts (2); four stages - preliminary written test, PMT, PET, final written exam (3); final selection strictly on relative merit
// (4). The petitioners, "equally meritorious", were not selected; provisional selectees were sent for antecedent verification and
// medical examination; some dropped out (selected in different departments, unwillingness to join, etc.), leaving 400 posts unfilled;
// the petitioners, the next meritorious and willing to join, asked to be considered (5). Not considered; the counter relied on
// Clause 33(ix) (no waiting list, the provisional list final) and said that, having taken part "with open eyes", they cannot question
// it (6). Clause 33(ix): "no waiting list ... under any circumstances"; left-over vacancies (adverse antecedents, medical unfitness,
// relinquishment, unwillingness, non-joining at induction training) "shall be deemed to have lapsed" (9); Clause 25: selection
// provisional, subject to verification (9). G.O.Ms.No.81 (22.02.1997) and the Ad hoc Rule of 04.12.1998 (G.O.Ms.No.544, Art. 309
// proviso): no waiting list, fall-out vacancies to the next recruitment (11); Munja Praveen, (2017) 14 SCC 797, Narimetla Vamsi
// (C.A. 4735/2022), K. Ashok (C.A. 209/2023): the rule applies only where the selectee relinquishes after appointment orders (12-16);
// "As the interpretation was not to the liking of the Respondents, Clause 33(ix) was introduced ... to overcome the Judgments" (17).
// Held: Clauses 25 and 33(ix) contradict each other (18); 9 months' induction training under the 1999 SCT Rules (19); the provisional
// selection is at a "nascent stage" (20); some justification for not going to the next in line after induction training, but "at the
// stage of verification, it would be unfair to deny consideration of the next meritorious in the line" (21); "appears to be an
// arbitrary condition" (22); "It is quite ironic ..." (23); the right to be considered for public employment is a fundamental right
// (24); no rationale in the counter beyond ease of closure; "arbitrary, irrational and violative of Article 14 and 16" (25); mere
// participation does not validate an arbitrary clause, Meeta Sahai (26); no cause of action earlier (27); Clause 33(ix) runs contrary
// to the Ad hoc Rule as the Supreme Court read it (28); Vallampati Satish Babu and Santhosh Kumar C distinguished (29-33). RELIEF:
// writ petitions DISPOSED OF with directions - (i) Clause 33(ix) "declared to be arbitrary and unfair" (not "struck down" or "read
// down"); (ii) the respondents "shall take steps for appointment in the left over vacancies ... by adhering to merit as well as
// reservations"; (iii) within four months from receipt of a copy of the order; (iv) no costs (34).
