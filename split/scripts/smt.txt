# 3 · THE SINGLE JUDGE
10 | The single judge quashed the refusal, noting her plea that promoted judges' children get it, and held denying a direct recruit's child was discrimination.
# 4 · WHAT THE COURT HELD
10 | The Division Bench held: a directly recruited Class One officer's child falls in the creamy layer. Both the parent's post and the mode of entry matter. || The Division Bench held: a directly recruited Class I officer's child falls in the creamy layer. Both the parent's post and the mode of entry matter.
10 | The two thousand four clarification covers only staff rising from Class Three or Four. An advocate recruited directly at forty one falls outside it. || The 2004 clarification covers only staff rising from Class III or IV. An advocate recruited directly at 41 falls outside it.
10 | A mere letter cannot override the Office Memorandum, per Rohith Nathan. Equality doesn't require treating different service categories alike.
# 5 · THE RESULT
10 | Sympathy, however genuine, cannot override the criteria, the Court said. The State's appeal was allowed, and the Tahsildar's refusal was upheld.
