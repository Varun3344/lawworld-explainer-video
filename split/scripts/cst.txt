# 5 · WHAT THE COURT HELD
10 | Finally, what did the High Court decide? The force majeure clause applies only if something stops the consumer from receiving power, like war, riot or a strike.
10 | Here, it did receive and use power, and no notice was given under that clause. So the appeal was allowed, and the single judge's order was set aside.
