// Taliparamba in C.C. No. 923 of 2006 (cause title, para 1); complaint under ss.11(1), 12(b) r/w s.33 BIS Act, 1986: the BIS Joint
// Director, Thiruvananthapuram, with three other officials inspected the factory and found the first accused (the partnership firm)
// possessing a roller with a spurious ISI mark, without a valid BIS licence; a spurious ISI mark also on the factory wall; the firm (A1)
// and its partners (A2-A5) arrayed (para 4); PW1-3, Exts.P1-P6, MO1 roller; the Magistrate: no cogent and believable evidence that the
// firm used a trade mark or ISI mark on any article, no finished product with the fake mark produced, the MO1 seizure not properly proved
// to show it in working condition and capable of applying the mark; all acquitted (para 5); the BIS argued mere possession of a spurious
// ISI mark suffices, the Magistrate wrongly required proof of use (para 6); the accused: mere possession for affixing on goods does not
// attract ss.11/12 r/w 33 (para 7). Held: in ss.11 and 12 "the legislature vigilantly incorporated the word 'used'" and "never intended
// the word 'possess' to fasten criminal culpability on mere possession of a spurious ISI mark"; no goods bearing the fake mark were
// detected, seized or produced; "use" is punishable, and "mere possession of an object capable of affixing such a mark, without any
// evidence to establish its use in the manufacture of goods", does not attract ss.11 and 12 r/w 33; the Magistrate was right (para 10);
// appeal dismissed, acquittal confirmed (11-12).
