// Facts used (para): the suit, under Article 131, claims a share of the power from the Bhakra-Nangal and Beas Hydro-Electric Projects (1);
// the dispute among the successor States, unresolved for decades, led to the suit in 1996 (2). The projects were built "substantially
// upon the territory of the then princely State of Bilaspur and within the Kangra district", both now in Himachal (5.1); the power was
// "enjoyed principally" by composite Punjab and, after 1966, by Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan and Chandigarh (5.2). Punjab reorganised from
// 01.11.1966: split into Punjab and Haryana, Chandigarh created, territory "together with its population" transferred to Himachal (5.8);
// Section 78 of the Punjab Reorganisation Act, 1966 (5.9); only ad hoc allocations (5.10). Himachal's claim of 22.10.1969: 7.19%, "founded
// upon the proportion of the population of composite Punjab that had been transferred" to it; rejected by the Centre in 1994; a 1995
// tripartite meeting failed (5.12); suit in 1996: 12% free power (Home State), 7.19%, Rs. 2,199.77 crores compensation, interest (5.13).
// Judgment of 27.09.2011: decreed in part, "entitled to 7.19% of the power generated" (3); not time-barred etc. (5.20); 12% free power
// refused (5.22); 7.19% of composite Punjab's power = 6.095% of Bhakra-Nangal from 01.11.1966, 5.752% of Beas Unit I and 2.984% of Beas
// Unit II from production (5.23); the Union to compute the amount and file it "within six months" (5.25). Ministry of Power order of
// 31.10.2011 revised the allocation from 01.11.2011 (5.27). Union's affidavit of 05.07.2013: 13,066 million units (8,523 + 3,145 + 1,398)
// for 01.11.1966 to October 2011; "the Plaintiff-State accepted this computation, the same was disputed by the States of Punjab and
// Haryana" (5.28). Settlement efforts through the Attorney General from 07.08.2014 (report 16.01.2015), the third reference from
// 11.09.2024, a "last opportunity" on 04.02.2025 (5.34, 5.35). Punjab: net Rs. 6,683.68 crores "recoverable from the Plaintiff-State as on
// 31.03.2026" (13, 14). The AG's cashless framework "without requiring any direct financial transaction between the States" (20, 21);
// Himachal and Haryana "expressed their agreement to the proposal in principle" on 30.07.2026 and 20.08.2026 (23); Punjab's three
// objections: the 1.2 lakh units a day (11,094 MU), conversion at the O&M rate of 79 paise (5,768 MU), 30 years instead of 15 (24-26).
