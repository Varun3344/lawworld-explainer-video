// Facts used: collaboration agreement of 13.09.2013 for the petitioner's property, No. 162 Vikram Enclave Extension, Village Pasonda,
// Ghaziabad; the complainant to build ground, upper ground, first and second floors and get the first and second floors with roof rights
// over the second (para 2); per the complainant, settlement of 01.02.2016 (Ex. CW-1/Q): he gave up his claim to the second floor and its
// roof rights for Rs 30 lakh, three cheques (3); three SBI cheques of Rs 10 lakh each (Nos. 315386-315388, 01.03.2016 / 03.03.2016); two
// returned for insufficiency of funds, No. 315387 with an objection concerning alteration in the amount (4); trial court conviction
// 10.07.2019, sentence 19.07.2019: ten months' SI, Rs 42 lakh compensation, two months' SI in default; ASJ Shahdara dismissed the
// appeal 30.01.2020 (1); revision pleas: cheques were security, to be returned on completion of construction (5); No. 315387 void
// under s.87 NI Act (6).
// Held: signatures and delivery admitted (10); ss.118(a) and 139 presumptions apply (9); attesting witness CW-2 supported the settlement
// and delivery (12); the evidence gives an independent basis, the presumptions are not rebutted (15, 25); No. 315387: words "Ten Lakh
// only" and figures correspond; no proof an existing entry was altered without consent; the bank's 'alteration in amount' endorsement
// "does not, by itself, establish an unauthorised material alteration rendering the cheque void under Section 87" (20, 22); conviction
// and Rs 42 lakh compensation affirmed (28, 30); sentence cut from ten to six months' SI: property-development arrangement culminating in
// a settlement, family circumstances, Rs 21 lakh deposited in appeal and revision; default sentence unchanged (29, 30).
