// Facts used (para): the defendant's appeal against the judgment and decree dated 29.01.2010 in O.S. No. 2124 of 2006, X Addl. Senior
// Civil Judge (Fast Track Court), City Civil Court, Hyderabad (2). Suit for specific performance of the agreement of sale dated
// 26.12.2005 for Rs. 10,00,000 (4). The plaintiff, a permanent resident of Hyderabad temporarily residing in the USA, paid Rs. 50,000
// advance; the balance Rs. 9,50,000 was payable by 20.01.2006 (5). Ex.A1 stipulates the balance "shall be paid on or before
// 20.01.2006, failing which the advance sale consideration of Rs.50,000/- shall be forfeited and the defendant shall be entitled to
// alienate the property to any third party" (22). His mother's letter of 16.01.2006 under Certificate of Posting (6; Ex.A2, 19, 25);
// letter 13.03.2006 (Ex.A3), telegram/legal notice 18.10.2006 calling the defendant to the Sub-Registrar on 19.10.2006 (Ex.A4), GPA
// 15.07.2008 (Ex.A8) - all after 20.01.2006 (6, 7, 12, 25). Reply notice 31.10.2006: the defendant cancelled the agreement for non-payment
// in time and forfeited the Rs. 50,000 (7, 8, 23). Trial court: plaintiff ready and willing; cancellation illegal; the forfeiture
// clause does not authorise cancellation, so time not the essence; decree: receive Rs. 9,50,000, execute the sale deed, deliver
// possession within 45 days (12, 13). Appellant: time was the essence; mere telegraphic notice does not establish readiness; no prayer
// to set aside the cancellation (14-16). Respondent: Exs.A1-A9 show readiness; no automatic cancellation, so time not the essence (19).
// HELD: the agreement "has to read as a whole": on non-payment by the date the seller may forfeit the advance and sell to others, so
// "the time was the essence of the contract" (22); unilateral cancellation without a stipulation is impermissible (K.S. Manjunath,
// 2025 INSC 1298) (23-24); s.16(c) SRA: only Rs. 50,000 of Rs. 10,00,000 paid; except Ex.A2 the plaintiff did not establish readiness
// within the stipulated time; Exs.A3, A4, A8 came after 20.01.2006 (25); "the plaintiff was never ready and willing to perform his
// part of the contract, particularly when he was residing in the USA"; Certificate of Posting "is also not a proper mode", service
// not proved, he ought to have sent it by registered post; Mohammed Khaleel v. Jayamma, 2026 INSC 651; "there is no concrete material
// placed by plaintiff to show that he was always ready and willing to perform his part" (27); trial court's findings not just and
// valid (28); specific performance is equitable and discretionary; where a substantial amount remains unpaid the Court considers the
// surrounding circumstances; only Rs. 50,000 of Rs. 10,00,000 paid (29); by paying a "paltry" Rs. 50,000 and prolonging the matter the
// plaintiff defeated the purpose of the sale (the defendant's case: urgent financial need) (30). ORDER: appeal allowed; the decree
// dated 29.01.2010 in O.S. 2124/2006 set aside; the suit dismissed (31, 32; alt 28: "No costs").
