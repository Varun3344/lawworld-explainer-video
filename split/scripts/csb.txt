# 4 · WHAT THE COURT HELD
10 | The Court held: reason to believe means cause or justification, tangible material with a live link to the belief. Sufficiency is not for the courts. || The Court held: "reason to believe" means cause or justification, tangible material with a live link to the belief. Sufficiency is not for the courts.
10 | A change of opinion needs an opinion first formed. Here, one brother's assessment was a bare acceptance of his return; the other got only an intimation.
# 5 · THE VERDICT
10 | Coming after the assessment, the report was fresh material. Under the law as it stood in twenty fifteen, the notices were valid.
