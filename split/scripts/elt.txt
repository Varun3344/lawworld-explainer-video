// Facts used (para): appeal under Section 37 of the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 against the Single Judge's decision of
// 02.05.2017 dismissing the Section 34 objection petition (1); the appellants invited tenders for tentage, furniture and electrical
// items on hire basis for the 1999 Lok Sabha General Elections; the respondent, the successful bidder, was awarded the work for the
// Karol Bagh and Outer Delhi Parliamentary Constituencies on 09.08.1999; agreement with an arbitration clause (2); bills of
// Rs.19,98,426 (Karol Bagh) and Rs.80,30,654 (Outer Delhi); the appellants alleged the bills were inflated; clause 19: payment subject
// to scrutiny, approval of bills and physical verification of the tentage before dismantling; show-cause notice of 31.03.2000 for not
// submitting daily challans verified by the Returning Officers (2.1); arbitration invoked by the respondent; sole arbitrator appointed
// by the court; claims Rs.1,00,28,788; the arbitrator "allowed the claims" and awarded Rs.1,00,28,788 with interest @9% p.a. on
// Rs.15,22,772 from February 2000, post-award interest @9% if unpaid within two months, and costs of Rs.2,00,000 (2.2); the Single
// Judge dismissed the Section 34 objections and upheld the award (2.3). Appellants: clause 19 had three prerequisites; only the RO's
// physical verification undisputed (3); the arbitrator failed to consider the Finance Department's objections that the bills were
// inflated: patent illegality, perversity (Associate Builders; DAMEPL) (3.1). Respondent: the contentions were never pressed under
// Section 34; reasons were recorded; plausible view (Jan De Nul, (2026) 3 SCC 186) (4); challans submitted and goods physically
// verified by the RO (4.1). Clause 19 (6, 7); no other CEO representative appointed; verification by the RO; orders oral (8). Under
// Section 34 only ground 'E' was pressed (10). HELD: "The issues not raised under Section 34 of the Act cannot be permitted to be raised
// in the appeal under Section 37 of the Act." (11); "Be that as it may, the contention that the arbitrator ignored the objections of
// the Finance Department is misconceived"; the tentage was supplied in the last quarter of 1999 and the bills physically verified by
// the RO; both ROs deposed the bills were signed after physical verification (12); the Finance Department's objections "were not found
// reliable" since the tentage was physically verified before dismantling, whereas the scrutiny started in July 2000 (13); the arbitrator
// held the RO's verification more reliable; the inquiry reports were prepared "much later, after dismantling of the structure"; the
// quantum of evidence is the arbitrator's exclusive domain (Ferro Concrete, (2009) 12 SCC 1) (14); the 2006 inquiry reports against two
// officials were rightly rejected by the Single Judge (15); "the view taken by the arbitrator is plausible, suffers from no factual or
// legal error, much less perversity and calls for no interference" (16); "the appeal is dismissed" (17).

# 5 · WHAT THE COURT HELD
10 | Finally, what did the High Court decide? Issues not raised before the Single Judge cannot be raised in this appeal. And the finance objections were not ignored.
