// Facts used (para): appeal under s.173 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 by the insurer against the award dated 20.12.2022 in M.C.O.P.
// 341/2020, Motor Accident Claims Tribunal (Principal District Judge), Pudukkottai (prayer, 1). The claimant worked as the driver of a
// Maruti car insured with the appellant; during the course of his employment he met with an accident on 22.01.2020, sustained severe
// injuries and was treated in hospital; he claimed compensation against the owner and the insurer (2.1). Insurer's counter: denied
// the manner of the accident and liability; no employer-employee relationship (2.2). Evidence: PW1, PW2, Exs. P1-P13; RW1, Exs. R1-R3;
// C1, C2 (2.3). Tribunal: liability on the insurer; the claimant was the driver; though filed under the MV Act, treated as a claim
// under the Workmen's Compensation Act; Rs.19,77,595 with interest at 7.25% p.a. from the petition to realisation (3.1). Insurer: no
// evidence to convert the claim; the Tribunal had no jurisdiction to convert it; the amount is not in accordance with the Employees'
// Compensation Act (4.1). Claimant: PW2 established that he was the driver, PW1 also deposed so, cogent and corroborative; amount
// properly calculated under the Employees' Compensation Act (5.1). Held: PW1 deposed he worked as the driver earning Rs.10,000 a
// month; PW2 deposed the owner asked him to arrange a driver and he arranged the claimant (7.1); nothing material elicited in PW2's
// cross-examination; proceedings are summary; the evidence "cogent, corroborative and trustworthy" and no contra evidence from the
// insurer (7.2); following the Division Bench in Oriental Insurance Co. Ltd. v. Krishnan, (2003) 2 LW 73 and Oriental Insurance Co.
// Ltd. v. Kaliya Pillai, (2002) 4 CTC 469: "when this Court finds that a claim under the Motor Vehicles Act is not maintainable, it is
// the duty of the Court to consider converting the claim into one under the Employees' Compensation Act and grant the lesser relief
// permissible thereunder, provided the facts establish that the claimant was employed by the owner of the vehicle and that the
// accident occurred in the course of such employment"; employment by the owner and the insurance proved; findings concurred (7.3);
// the plea that the claimant was not covered under any category of the policy rejected (7.4). ORDER: C.M.A. dismissed; the award of
// 20.12.2022 confirmed; no costs; C.M.P. closed (8.1).

# 5 · WHAT THE COURT HELD
10 | Finally, what did the High Court decide? The witnesses were cogent and trustworthy, and the insurer led no contrary evidence. He was the car owner's driver.
