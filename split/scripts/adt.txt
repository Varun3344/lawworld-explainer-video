// Facts used (para): writ petition to quash the order dated 24.11.2023 passed by the Commissioner, CGST, Delhi South Commissionerate (1);
// the petitioner was a registered dealer with the Service Tax Department; a show cause notice (SCN) was issued; reply filed on
// 23.05.2022; personal hearing afforded on 07.11.2023; adjourned to 15.11.2023 at the petitioner's request; on 15.11.2023 it sought
// further time till 24.11.2023 to produce the requisite documents; the request was not considered and the order was passed on
// 24.11.2023 (2). Petitioner: no reasonable opportunity to defend; by email dated 15.11.2023 it sought a further period of ten days,
// and the order was passed without considering the request (3). Department: sufficient opportunities; hearing on 07.11.2023 and time
// till 15.11.2023 to place additional material, but no attempt; no document filed till 23.11.2023, only then the order on 24.11.2023 (4);
// counter para 5.6: the authorities "waited for an additional period of 9 days" after 15.11.2023 (8). Held: the "after waiting for nine
// days" contention "lacks merit": once the request was not considered there was no occasion to file the documents, and the order was
// passed on 24.11.2023 whereas the time sought was till 24.11.2023 (6); an alternative remedy of appeal exists, but the self-imposed
// restriction is subject to the Whirlpool Corpn. v. Registrar of Trade Marks, (1998) 8 SCC 1 exceptions, one being "a violation of the
// principle of natural justice" (7); a reasonable opportunity to defend was not granted; the request to produce material by 24.11.2023
// "was neither considered nor rejected" (8); the email request "was not even noted" in the impugned order; one of the grounds for the
// order was the failure to produce the relevant documents; "The minimum that was expected from a quasi-judicial authority was at least

// order "is violative of principles of natural justice and is set aside"; remanded to respondent no.1 (the Commissioner) to decide
