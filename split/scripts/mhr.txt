// Facts used (para): both petitioners are partnership firms at the Antharasanahalli Industrial Area, KIADB, Tumkur, trading as
// stockists / CNF agents for pan masala and chewing tobacco (3, 9). On 19.08.2025 Vadgaon Police Station (Kolhapur District,
// Maharashtra) intercepted a truck at Kagni Toll Naka, Hatkanangale, Kolhapur, on the Bengaluru-Pune highway (5, 10); on 20.08.2025 a
// constable's complaint against unknown persons -> Crime No. 446/2025 under the FSS Act 2006 and BNS ss.275, 274, 223, 123 (6, 10,
// 20.15). The petitioners are not named in the FIR (7, 11, 20.15). On 21.08.2025 five to six Vadgaon police personnel visited the
// premises at Tumkur; notices of that date required the partners to appear at Vadgaon, but their statements were recorded at Tumkur
// (7, 11, 20.15). Counsel: s.179 BNSS (= s.160 CrPC) confines the power to require attendance to persons "within the limits of his own
// or any adjoining station" (13.3-13.5, 20.2); no Karnataka police were present (13.2); the books of account were inspected and
// ledger copies taken (13.6, 21.1). Respondents 1-2 (Maharashtra, Vadgaon PS) served, did not appear (20.11, 21.9). The AGA for
// respondent 3 (Karnataka, Tumkur Rural PS), on instructions: the Vadgaon police never contacted them; not informed before, during or
// after (16.2, 21.10); the Court accepted it (21.12). Held: s.179 imposes a real territorial limit; the petitioners in Tumkur are not
// within the limits of Vadgaon PS or an adjoining station; the notices, so far as they required attendance at Vadgaon, were without
// authority (20.18-20.37, 20.59); the finding is limited, the FIR is not challenged, no finding on the merits (20.57). The Tumkur
// operation (statements, inspection, ledger books or copies) was without the required coordination with the Karnataka police and
// without shown compliance with ss.185-186 BNSS: "unlawful" (21.43, 21.85, 21.95, 21.98); the statements are not to be relied on as
// lawfully obtained, though fresh statements may be taken in accordance with law (21.92, 21.96); no finding on motive (21.94).
// Standing Order 576 (1962) binds only the Karnataka police (22.14). Relief limited; the FIR is not quashed and the investigation may
// continue in accordance with law (23.17-23.20, 24.4). ORDER: both petitions partly allowed; notices dated 21.08.2025 unenforceable so
// far as they require appearance at Vadgaon under s.179, no coercive action for non-compliance (ii); no such notices while the
// petitioners are outside that station and any adjoining one (iii); the inter-State operation at Tumkur declared unsustainable in law,

// prayers to restrain all contact and to quash the FIR / investigation declined (viii); no finding on the merits or anyone's guilt (ix).
