# 1 · WHAT A TEMPORARY INJUNCTION DOES
6 | Construction starts tomorrow on land you claim. Your suit may take years. Can a court freeze things today?
10 | Hi, I'm Ravishankar from LawWorld. That is what a temporary injunction under Order Thirty Nine of the Code of Civil Procedure is for.
8 | It is an interim order made while a suit is pending, preserving the position until the rights are decided.
6 | Section Ninety Four, clause C, gives the power. Order Thirty Nine sets out how it is used.
6 | A perpetual injunction, by contrast, is granted only by the decree, on the merits.
# 2 · WHEN RULES 1 AND 2 APPLY
8 | A hypothetical case. Anita sues her neighbour Naresh over a strip of land, and he starts digging foundations there.
10 | Rule One covers disputed property in danger of being wasted, damaged or alienated, or wrongfully sold in execution of a decree.
8 | It covers a defendant who threatens or intends to remove or dispose of his property to defraud his creditors.
8 | Or threats to dispossess the plaintiff, or cause other injury in relation to the disputed property. That fits Anita.
8 | Rule Two covers suits to restrain a breach of contract or other injury, on terms such as giving security.
# 3 · THE THREE TESTS
8 | Fitting a situation is only the start. In Dalpat Kumar versus Prahlad Singh, the Supreme Court explained three tests.
10 | First, a prima facie case: a bona fide, substantial question for trial. It is not prima facie title; title must be proved by evidence at the trial.
10 | Second, irreparable injury: a material injury that damages cannot adequately compensate. A prima facie case alone is not enough.
8 | Third, the balance of convenience: the court compares the mischief to each side if relief is refused, or if granted.
8 | Anita's sale deed raises a serious question, and a short pause may cost Naresh less than a permanent structure would cost her.
# 4 · CONDUCT MATTERS
6 | Even then, the relief is discretionary and equitable, so the applicant's conduct counts.
8 | In Gujarat Bottling versus Coca Cola, the Supreme Court said that conduct should be fair and honest, and free from blame.
6 | Hiding a material fact, or waiting months while the walls rise, can count against Anita.
# 5 · WITHOUT NOTICE: EX PARTE ORDERS
6 | Now suppose Naresh plans to pour the concrete tomorrow morning. Must the court hear him first?
8 | Rule Three makes notice to the other side the norm, unless the delay would defeat the very object of the injunction.
8 | Acting without notice, the court must record its reasons. In Morgan Stanley, the Supreme Court called such orders exceptional.
8 | Anita must at once deliver, or send by registered post, copies of her application, affidavit, plaint and documents.
6 | That day or the next, she must file an affidavit that the copies were delivered or sent.
8 | Under Rule Three A, the court must try to finally decide the application within thirty days, or record why it could not. || Under Rule 3A, the court must try to finally decide the application within 30 days, or record why it could not.
# 6 · CHALLENGING THE ORDER
8 | Naresh can respond. Under Rule Four, any dissatisfied party may apply to discharge, vary or set aside the order.
10 | An ex parte order resting on a knowingly false or misleading material statement must be vacated, unless the court records reasons not to.
# 7 · IF THE ORDER IS DISOBEYED
8 | If Naresh keeps building, Rule Two A lets the court attach his property and detain him in civil prison for up to three months. || If Naresh keeps building, Rule 2A lets the court attach his property and detain him in civil prison for up to three months.
8 | Attachment cannot last beyond one year. If the breach continues, the property may be sold, and the injured party compensated.
# 8 · APPEALS
8 | Orders under Rules One, Two, Two A, Four and Ten are appealable under Order Forty Three, Rule One, clause R. || Orders under Rules 1, 2, 2A, 4 and 10 are appealable under Order XLIII Rule 1(r).
8 | But in Wander versus Antox, the Supreme Court said an appeal court should not substitute its own discretion for the trial court's.
10 | It interferes only if discretion was exercised arbitrarily, capriciously or perversely, or settled principles were ignored.
# 9 · MANDATORY RELIEF AND LEGAL BARS
8 | Suppose the wall is already up, and Anita wants it pulled down now. That is a mandatory interim injunction.
8 | In Dorab Cawasji Warden, the Supreme Court required a strong case for trial, of a higher standard than a prima facie case.
8 | It also needs irreparable or serious injury, and the balance of convenience in the applicant's favour.
8 | Section Forty One of the Specific Relief Act lists cases where no injunction can be granted. Its bars reach interim relief too.
# 10 · DRAFTING THE APPLICATION
8 | When drafting, prove each test with documents and an affidavit, since Rule One speaks of proof by affidavit or otherwise.
8 | Seeking it without notice? Show the urgency, disclose every material fact, and finish the Rule Three steps on time.
6 | Finally, check any local High Court amendments to Order Thirty Nine before you file.
