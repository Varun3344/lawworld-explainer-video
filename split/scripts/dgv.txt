// Facts used: the consumer was accused of an offence under s.135 of the Electricity Act, 2003; on 03.01.2024 the distribution licensee
// served a notice titled a supplementary bill: Rs 92,99,077.55 towards theft and Rs 23,45,845 compounding charges, prosecution under
// s.135 if unpaid (4(i)); a Single Judge of the High Court of Gujarat (at Ahmedabad) on 12.02.2024 permitted deposit of 50% of the bill
// incl. reconnection charges and directed reconnection on deposit (4(ii), fn 3); licensee's LPA No. 592 of 2024 (4(iii)); the Division
// Bench (25.06.2024) let the consumer file a reply / all objections before the Executive Engineer within two weeks, bill, deposits and
// reconnection subject to his decision, and an appeal under s.127 if it went against him (4(iv)); the connected appeal: bill of
// 01.06.2023, Single Judge 50% (23.01.2024), Division Bench remitted (25.04.2024) (5); licensee: theft assessment differs from s.126, no
// objection, no s.127 appeal, the Special Court fixes civil liability under s.154(5) (7, 8); 50% "too lenient" (38).
// Held: no provision for assessment other than s.126, no separate Assessing Officer for theft; the "assessed amount" in the third proviso
// to s.135(1A) is assessed by the s.126 procedure (provisional assessment, objection, hearing - natural justice) until the Supply Code
// prescribes one (31, 34, 37(i)); subject to a s.127 appeal; the appellate order final only for restoration; where a criminal complaint
// is filed it does not bind the Special Court on civil liability (s.154(5)); excess refunded (s.154(6)) (35-37); theft cases: "a
// circumspect approach is required", the High Court may even decline restoration till the entire provisionally assessed amount is paid
// (39); where the provisional assessment is prima facie arbitrary, restoration on not less than 50%, matching s.127(2) (40); the High
// Court was "well within its jurisdiction"; clarified: subject to a s.127 appeal and to civil liability fixed by the Special Court;
// appeal dismissed; the connected appeal (a remand) dismissed too (41, 41).
// Caution: theft is only alleged / assessed - never said that anyone stole. The consumer is never named in a spoken line (Flow policy);

# 3 · THE DISPUTE

# 4 · WHAT THE COURT HELD
10 | The Supreme Court held: the Act has no assessment provision except Section One Twenty Six. So the amount to deposit for reconnection follows that same procedure. || The Supreme Court held: the Act has no assessment provision except Section 126. So the amount to deposit for reconnection follows that same procedure.
10 | That means a provisional assessment, a hearing on objections, and an appeal. Where a complaint is filed, the Special Court decides civil liability.
# 5 · THE VERDICT
10 | Theft cases demand caution. But if the provisional assessment is prima facie arbitrary, the High Court may restore supply on at least half. Both appeals failed.
