# 4 · WHAT THE COURT HELD
10 | The Court held this clause is not an unrestricted licence to use a registered mark just by calling it descriptive. Context decides: prominence, placement, size.
10 | Greenmount's house mark does not, by itself, answer infringement, nor does peaceful co-existence. What counts is how the average consumer sees the mark and the whole product.
