# 1 · REOPEN A SETTLED POINT?
10 | Hi, I am Ravishankar from LawWorld. Can a tribunal, on review, reopen a point the Supreme Court had already settled, five years after its own order?
10 | On thirtieth October, a two-judge Supreme Court bench of Honourable Justice J B Pardiwala and Honourable Justice K Vinod Chandran ruled. || On 30 October, a two-judge Supreme Court bench of Honourable Justice J.B. Pardiwala and Honourable Justice K. Vinod Chandran ruled.
# 2 · THE TARIFF
10 | The regulator set Damodar Valley Corporation's tariff for two thousand nine to twenty fourteen. A sinking fund to redeem its bonds counted as an expense. || The regulator set Damodar Valley Corporation's tariff for 2009 to 2014. A sinking fund to redeem its bonds counted as an expense.
10 | The Supreme Court upheld this for earlier years, in Bhaskar Shrachi. In twenty nineteen, relying on it, the Appellate Tribunal upheld the tariff. No one appealed. || The Supreme Court upheld this for earlier years, in Bhaskar Shrachi. In 2019, relying on it, the Appellate Tribunal upheld the tariff. No one appealed.
# 3 · THE REVIEW
10 | A consumer's review waited five years. Then the Tribunal allowed it, calling depreciation on bond-funded assets plus the sinking fund a double allowance.
10 | It said the point had escaped even the Supreme Court. The Corporation appealed, and the Steel Authority of India intervened to back the review.
# 4 · WHAT THE COURT HELD
10 | Had Bhaskar Shrachi erred, the Court could refer it to a larger bench. But it had weighed depreciation with the sinking fund, so there was no scope for a review.
10 | Under Kamal Sengupta, the error must be evident on the record, and a review cannot sit in appeal. Here, the Court said, the Tribunal has gone haywire. || Under Kamal Sengupta, the error must be evident on the record, and a review cannot sit in appeal. Here, the Court said, "the Tribunal has gone haywire."
# 5 · THE VERDICT
10 | Clearly illegal, the review breached Article One Forty One. The twenty nineteen order was restored, with costs on that consumer and the Steel Authority. || Clearly illegal, the review breached Article 141. The 2019 order was restored, with costs on that consumer and the Steel Authority.
