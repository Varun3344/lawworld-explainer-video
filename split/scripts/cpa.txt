// Facts used (page / para): appeal by the Kalyana Karnataka Road Transport Corporation against the single judge's order of
// 19.08.2025 in W.P. 101661/2025, which directed consideration of the brother's claim to compassionate appointment notwithstanding
// the Circular (para 1). The employee served the Corporation as a Driver from 18.03.2008 until his death on 21.09.2023 (paras 3.1,
// 9). His mother applied for compassionate appointment, not for herself but for her other son (3.1, 10). His wife had predeceased
// him on 09.04.2022; no children (9). Mother about 65, brother about 34; the brother is married and resides separately with his own
// family; he filed an affidavit undertaking to look after and maintain the mother (10). Rejection by endorsement dated 04.11.2024
// on the ground that the deceased was married (single judge's order quoted at para 11, its para 2). The single judge: if the spouse
// predeceased the employee and there are no children, the mere marriage of the deceased cannot be a ground to reject; directed the
// Corporation to consider and appoint the brother to a suitable post within twelve weeks; liberty to the mother to seek
// cancellation if he does not take care of her (para 11, its paras 9 and ORDER iii-iv). Corporation: the Circular defines dependents
// with precision; the brother, married and residing separately, is not a dependent; compassionate appointment is an exception to the
// constitutional mandate of equality in public employment (paras 4, 5). Respondents: the soul of compassionate appointment is
// rescuing a family from the sudden financial catastrophe after the breadwinner's death; mother and brother looked to him; the
// brother's affidavit (paras 6, 7). The Circular: married male employee -> widow and children (dependent, living with him);
// unmarried male employee -> a dependent brother or sister residing with him (paras 14, 15). Held: "Marriage is a fact in the life
// of the employee; the death of the spouse does not obliterate that fact"; widowerhood cannot be judicially equated with never
// having been married (paras 16, 17); "Compassion is the foundation of the scheme; eligibility is its boundary" (18); "Judicial
// sympathy cannot rewrite an administrative policy" (20); an undertaking to maintain the mother cannot elevate him into an eligible
// dependent (21, 22); "Compassionate appointment cannot become compassionate recruitment" (25); "a concession born of necessity, not
// a right born of relationship" (26). ORDER: writ appeal allowed; single judge's order set aside; the Corporation's rejection
// sustained; the observations are confined to compassionate appointment and do not prejudice any other benefit, if otherwise
// admissible, to the legal heirs (para 28).
// Not said: the names of the employee, his mother, his brother or his late wife (private persons). Flow policy: no accusation, no
// "fraud" / "collusion" / "admitted"; only the court, the judges, the corporation and the rule are named.
# 1 · WHO GETS THE JOB?
10 | A driver with a State bus corporation dies in service. His wife had died before him, and there were no children. Can his brother now get a job on compassionate grounds?
10 | His mother sought a job for her other son, who promised to look after her. The corporation said no, but a single judge of the Karnataka High Court said yes.

# 2 · THE FAMILY LEFT BEHIND
10 | He joined in two thousand eight. His wife died in twenty twenty two; he died in twenty twenty three. His brother is married, with his own family, living apart. || He joined in 2008. His wife died in 2022; he died in 2023. His brother is married, with his own family, living apart.
10 | The corporation's rule covers a married employee's widow and children. A brother qualifies only if the employee was unmarried. So it rejected the claim.
10 | The single judge held that when the wife died first and there are no children, mere marriage cannot bar the claim, and ordered the corporation to appoint him within twelve weeks.
# 3 · COMPASSION OR THE RULE?
10 | The corporation appealed: such jobs are an exception to equal public employment. The family said the scheme is meant to rescue families who lose their earner.

# 5 · WHAT THE COURT HELD
10 | Finally, what did the High Court decide? Marriage is a fact in an employee's life; a wife's death does not erase it. Under this rule, a widower is not unmarried.
10 | A promise to care for the mother cannot create eligibility. So the appeal was allowed, the single judge's order set aside and the rejection upheld.
