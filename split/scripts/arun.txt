10 | The petitioner came to this Court for bail after six years in custody as an undertrial, in a case resting entirely on circumstantial evidence.
10 | He was never named in the F I R or the Section One Sixty One statement. C C T V footage and a co-accused's confession made him an accused. || He was never named in the FIR or the Section 161 statement. CCTV footage and a co-accused's confession made him an accused.

# 4 · WHAT WENT WRONG
10 | None of four circumstances was proved: ransom claim, confessions, last seen, electronic evidence. Reading that judgment, the Court was appalled at the investigation.
10 | The remains were not identified by D N A. The motorcycle was not produced. C C T V and call records had no Section Sixty Five B certificate. || The remains were not identified by DNA. The motorcycle was not produced. CCTV and call records had no Section 65B certificate.
# 5 · SIX YEARS, SIX LAKH
10 | Six years were erased from the life of a man, the Court said, on mere surmises and conjectures. The State Police would do well to educate their officers.
10 | He was a victim of the system. The State must pay him six lakh rupees within one month, with eighteen per cent a year interest if late. The petition was disposed of. || He was a victim of the system. The State must pay him ₹6 lakh within one month, with 18% a year interest if late. The petition was disposed of.
