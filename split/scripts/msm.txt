// Facts used: Clause 15 Letters Patent appeal against the Single Judge's order of 18.10.2024 in W.P. No. 10688 of 2024 (prayer), which

// sets across Primary Health Centres in Tamil Nadu, repeat order for 4 more on 22.10.2009 (2.1); "It is stated": bills raised for the
// full value; the Corporation deducted Rs.19,71,049/- as liquidated damages, "citing delayed delivery schedules" (2.2); "It is averred":
// the supplier invoked Section 18 (Claim Petition No. 26 of 2012); on failure of conciliation the Council entered upon the reference as an
// arbitral tribunal; award 28.11.2016: refund of the withheld principal with statutory compound interest under Section 16 (2.3); the
// Section 34 (read with Section 19 MSMED Act) challenge before the Commercial Court, Kanpur Nagar, Arb. Misc. Case No. 274 of 2023,
// dismissed on 30.10.2023 (2.4); "Pursuant thereto", an Article 226 writ seeking to quash the award; the Single Judge dismissed it on
// 18.10.2024 (2.5); a Division Bench stayed further proceedings on the award on 16.07.2025, extended 24.09.2025 (2.6); the Corporation
// (AAG): jurisdictional flaws and procedural infirmities, so a writ lies to correct fundamental jurisdictional errors (3.1); the
// Section 34 petition was dismissed solely on delay; Tamil Nadu Cements (SLP (C) Diary No. 3776 of 2023, 22.01.2025): Larger Bench
// reference on writs against Council orders and a conciliating Council acting as arbitrator; keep the award in abeyance (3.2); the
// supplier: a Section 18(3) award has the status of an award under the 1996 Act; after Section 34, the remedy is a Section 37 appeal
// (4.1); having invoked Section 34 and lost, the Corporation cannot now plead the Council's order is not an award; approbate and
// reprobate; merger into the Kanpur order; no cause of action within this court's jurisdiction (4.2); Tamil Nadu Cements distinguishable
// (4.3); the two issues (5). Held: under Section 18(3)/(4) the award has "the identical legal status, efficacy and binding force as an
// arbitral award passed under the 1996 Act" (6); the 1996 Act is a self-contained code; the Corporation consciously filed Section 34
// at the competent Commercial Court, Kanpur Nagar, and raised a Section 29A ground (which applies only to an arbitral award); "A party
// cannot call the order an award for one purpose and a nullity for another."; after the adverse order of 30.10.2023 its recourse was
// "exhaustively confined to filing an appeal under Section 37" (7); a writ against an award that merged into the Kanpur order, when
// the remedy is a Section 37 appeal, would subvert the speedy-resolution intent of both Acts (8); the Single Judge's view affirmed;
// Article 226 cannot be invoked "as a substitute for a statutory appeal under Section 37"; appeal dismissed; the order of 18.10.2024
// confirmed; no costs; C.M.P.s closed; "the interim order granted is vacated" (9).
