// Held: a son's son is not a Class I heir, the pre-1956 right of a son's son in his grandfather's property during his father's lifetime
// was not saved (34); on the grandfather's death the property devolved on his Class I heirs under Section 8 and "did not constitute HUF

# 5 · WHAT THE COURT HELD
10 | Finally, what did the Supreme Court decide? What a father inherits under Section Eight of the Hindu Succession Act is his own, not joint family property.
10 | The partition made the father's share his exclusive property. The grandfather had died before the son was born, so the son could not claim a share. The appeal was dismissed.
