// Facts used: the writ challenges the DM Rajnandgaon's order dated 06.09.2021 rejecting the petitioner's s.14 SARFAESI application (1);
// respondents 1 & 2 (borrowers) borrowed Rs.7,96,388 and mortgaged their house (Khasra No. 101/5, Rajnandgaon), agreement 24.05.2017;

// secured asset has been sold to respondent no. 03 (Subsequent Purchaser), vide registered sale deed dated 13.12.2019" (2); the
// lender: the DM erred by entering the merits; s.14 powers are "purely ministerial in nature and not adjudicatory" (R.D. Jain & Co.
// v. Capital First, (2023) 1 SCC 675, para 25) (3); a transfer to a third party by the mortgagor does not oust the secured creditor's
// right; set aside and remand (4); respondent 3 supports the order (5); the State: rightly passed as the property was sold (6); the
// question - can the DM under s.14 enter the merits and adjudicate disputed questions of the parties' rights (8); the DM has no
// jurisdiction to adjudicate the merits or disputed facts; such issues fall within the DRT's jurisdiction (9); the later transfer to a
// third party could not by itself give the DM jurisdiction to adjudicate its validity or effect (11); the DM's role under s.14 "is
// ministerial in nature"; not justified in entering the merits on the basis of the subsequent sale (12a); Balkrishna Rama Tarle v.
// Phoenix ARC, (2023) 1 SCC 662, paras 18-19: the CMM/DM is not to adjudicate disputes between the borrower and the secured creditor

// Calcutta HC, Only Wheel v. Bank of Baroda, 2025 SCC OnLine Cal 7217: administrative / executive functions (12b); the DM "travelled
