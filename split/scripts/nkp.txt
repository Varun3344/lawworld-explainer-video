# 2 · KARGIL
10 | A soldier served at Kargil from two thousand three to two thousand five, under Operation Rakshak. There, he was diagnosed with rheumatoid arthritis. || A soldier served at Kargil from 2003 to 2005, under Operation Rakshak. There, he was diagnosed with rheumatoid arthritis.
10 | Discharged in twenty thirteen with a disability pension, he sought war injury pension from the Armed Forces Tribunal about six years later. || Discharged in 2013 with a disability pension, he sought war injury pension from the Armed Forces Tribunal about six years later.
# 3 · THE CLAIM
10 | His claim relied on a Defence policy clause: operations specially notified by the Government. The Tribunal allowed it, and the High Court upheld that.
10 | The Union argued the clause covers only operations like Snow Leopard, not ongoing ones like Operation Rakshak, in Jammu and Kashmir. The Court disagreed.
# 4 · WHAT THE COURT HELD
10 | Once an operation is notified, the clause applies, unless the policy or the notification says otherwise. Here, only the Union's oral statement did.
10 | The burden is on the policymaker, and the Court said: what a policy says, it is, and not what is in the imagination of the policymaker. || The burden is on the policymaker, and the Court said: "What a policy says, it is and not what is in the imagination of the policymaker."
# 5 · THE VERDICT
10 | Unrestrictive pension policies get a liberal reading. The appeal was dismissed, and future notifications must say clearly whether this pension applies.
