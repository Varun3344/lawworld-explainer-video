# 1 · UNSIGNED COPY?
10 | Hi, I am Ravishankar from LawWorld. The other side sends your client an unsigned copy of an arbitral award. Does the time to challenge it start running?
10 | On twenty sixth October, twenty twenty six, the Kerala High Court, through a single bench of Honourable Justice Mohammed Nias C P, answered that question. || On 26 October 2026, the Kerala High Court, through a single bench of Honourable Justice Mohammed Nias C.P., answered that question.
# 2 · THE CASE
10 | An arbitrator passed an award on twenty sixth March, twenty twenty four. The losing party challenged it before the Kottayam court under Section Thirty Four. || An arbitrator passed an award on 26 March 2024. The losing party challenged it before the Kottayam court under Section 34.
10 | On eighth April, he received an unsigned copy, posted by the award holder's lawyer. The signed copy came from the arbitrator only on twenty third June. || On 8 April, he received an unsigned copy, posted by the award holder's lawyer. The signed copy came from the arbitrator only on 23 June.
# 3 · THE QUESTION
10 | The award holder said the challenge was late: time ran from April, as the other side knew the award's contents. The Kottayam court disagreed, so he came to the High Court.
10 | The question: under Section Thirty Four, sub section Three, does the three month limit run from that unsigned copy, or only from receipt of the signed award? || The question: under Section 34(3), does the three-month limit run from that unsigned copy, or only from receipt of the signed award?
# 4 · WHAT STARTS THE CLOCK
10 | The High Court held: Section Thirty One needs a signed award, and a signed copy delivered to each party. Mere knowledge of its contents does not start limitation. || The High Court held: Section 31 needs a signed award, and a signed copy delivered to each party. Mere knowledge of its contents does not start limitation.
10 | Following Supreme Court rulings like Tecco Trichy and Ark Builders, it held: a copy from the opposite party starts no clock, under Section Thirty Four or Thirty Three. || Following Supreme Court rulings like Tecco Trichy and Ark Builders, it held: a copy from the opposite party starts no clock, under Section 34 or 33.
# 5 · THE VERDICT
10 | So time ran from twenty third June, and the challenge was in time. The award holder's petition was dismissed. Advocates, count from receipt of the signed award. || So time ran from 23 June, and the challenge was in time. The award holder's petition was dismissed. Advocates, count from receipt of the signed award.
