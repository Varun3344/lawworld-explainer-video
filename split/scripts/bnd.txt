// Facts used (para): two Article 32 petitions on identification, rescue, release and rehabilitation of bonded labourers (2); the
// first by a survivor of bonded labour, on the delays in immediate financial assistance (2); the second by the National Campaign
// Committee for the Eradication of Bonded Labour and others, seeking uniform and enforceable procedures (3). He was rescued with
// several other bonded labourers from a brick kiln in District Shahjahanpur, Uttar Pradesh, on 28.02.2019; they had been taken from
// Bihar "on the pretext of repayment of a bonded debt"; repatriated to Bihar and issued release certificates (5). Several did not
// receive the immediate financial assistance under the Central Sector Scheme for Rehabilitation of Bonded Labourers; he received it
// only in 2022, "nearly three years after his rescue" (6, 18). The petitioners' figures (a party's, voiced as "the petitioners say"):
// of 42 with release certificates only 25 got Rs. 20,000 each, 17 still waiting (6, rejoinder); "It was also submitted ... that 17
// labourers ... had still not received" (18, 30). Bihar: some UP release certificates not in the prescribed format (7). Orders of
// 19.09.2022, 30.08.2024 (NHRC), 21.11.2024 (Labour Secretary to meet all States/UTs) (8, 9); AG's note 21.04.2026; Union's compliance
// affidavit 15.05.2026 (10); revised SOP dated 14.05.2026 (25). Reasoning: "rehabilitation delayed may, in substance, amount to
// rehabilitation denied" (18); inter-State delays "frequently arise from inadequate coordination between the concerned authorities";
// a uniform framework with definite timelines is necessary (19); assistance not to be withheld merely because investigation or a
// criminal case is pending (21). Directions: the Union to ensure uniform implementation of the revised SOP throughout the country;
// all States/UTs to implement it and comply within six months; supplementary guidelines with the NHRC within three months (25);
// complaints verified ordinarily within 24 hours; release certificate ordinarily within 24 hours of the determination; a uniform
// format recognised by every State/UT (26); immediate financial assistance "shall ordinarily be disbursed within seven days of
// issuance of the release certificate, subject to necessary verification"; rehabilitation plan within 90 days; not withheld merely
// because investigation or criminal proceedings remain pending (27); the Union to decide on revising the scheme within three months
// (28); nodal officer per State/UT; no inter-State dispute shall delay payment (29); UP and Bihar to jointly verify the 17 within one
// month, pay any amount found payable preferably within three months, in any event within six (30); Vigilance Committees (31);
