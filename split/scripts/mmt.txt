// Facts used (para): Section 37 appeal against the Single Judge's judgment of 12.06.2023 dismissing the Section 34 petition (1); MMTC
// is a public sector undertaking; the respondent an Australian company exporting agricultural commodities; tender 30.03.2009 for

// arbitration, three-member tribunal (2.3); majority award signed 05.03.2021, dissent signed 16.06.2021, both sent 22.06.2021 (2.4);
// the majority rejected MMTC's claim 1 (delayed-shipment compensation, USD 861,963); allowed claim 2 (despatch money USD 30,620 +
// 9%); allowed counter-claim 1 (refund of the PBG, USD 271,950, with 9% from 12.01.2010); rejected counter-claim 2 (interest on the

// irregularities not material, plausible view (2.6). MMTC: award not signed by all; dissent signed after "a gap of 103 days"; "two
// separate awards" (3); clause 20 rebate needs no proof of loss (3.1); contradiction: counter-claim 2 rejected, yet 9% interest on the
// PBG granted in the conclusion (3.2); delay (3.3). Respondent: waiver (rule 58), majority prevails (rule 61), the dissent only an
// opinion (4.1); MMTC sold at a higher price, no loss proved, extension by mutual consent (4.2); no inconsistency, the interest

// majority award, the third a separate dissent, "not one of a missing signature" (10); "a difference of opinion among the members does

// the tribunal" (12); Dakshin Haryana distinguished, Section 29A does not apply to an international commercial arbitration (13); no

// refund (24); "the appeal is partly allowed. The impugned award is set aside to the limited extent of awarding interest at 9% per
// annum on the PBG." (25); pending applications disposed of (26).

# 5 · WHAT THE COURT HELD
10 | Finally, what did the High Court decide? A dissent does not create a second award. The majority award, signed by two members, is the tribunal's decision.
10 | But the contract barred interest on the guarantee. On these facts, that interest conflicted with India's public policy, so only that part was set aside.
