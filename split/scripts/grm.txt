# 3 · THE DEFENCE
10 | Its case: the trademark is Greenmount, and the other words only describe the product, a use protected by Section Thirty, sub section Two, clause A, of the Trade Marks Act. || Its case: the trademark is Greenmount, and the other words only describe the product, a use protected by Section 30(2)(a) of the Trade Marks Act.
# 4 · WHAT THE COURT HELD
10 | The Court held: calling a registered word descriptive is no unrestricted licence. What matters is whether, in context, it genuinely describes the goods.
10 | Equally, registration alone doesn't prove infringement. And Greenmount's house mark, or peaceful co existence, is no answer by itself. These are matters for trial. || Equally, registration alone doesn't prove infringement. And Greenmount's house mark, or peaceful co-existence, is no answer by itself. These are matters for trial.
