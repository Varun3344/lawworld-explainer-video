// Facts used (para): Section 37 appeal against the Single Judge's judgment of 14.12.2018 partially allowing IRCON's Section 34 objection
// petition (1); IRCON invited tenders for the construction of corporate office buildings at C-4, District Centre, Saket, New Delhi;
// CRS the successful bidder; Letter of Acceptance 19.07.2004; agreement 17.08.2004 with an arbitration clause (2); arbitration invoked by
// CRS; award dated 27.02.2018 (2.1); CRS raised eight claims, partially succeeded in two and was awarded cost of Rs.5.5 lakhs; claim 1
// (work done but not paid) Rs.2,70,25,343 claimed, Rs.11,83,921.94 awarded; claim 3 (retention / security money) Rs.31,37,844 awarded;
// claim 7: 9% simple interest p.a. from January 2010; claim 8 (litigation cost) Rs.5.50 lakhs; total Rs.48,71,765.94 (2.2); IRCON's
// Section 34 petition challenged claim 1 and the cost; allowed: the award "to the extent of directing payment of Rs.9,28,205/- under
// claim no.1 and cost of Rs.5.5 lakhs was set aside" (2.3). CRS: the Single Judge exceeded the scope of Section 34 and substituted the
// plausible view of the arbitrator (Konkan Railway, (2023) 9 SCC 85) (3); the final bill and deductions were in dispute, IRCON failed to
// defend them (3.1); detailed reasons for costs, Section 31A discretion rightly exercised (Om Vajrakaya, 2021:DHC:4273) (3.2). IRCON:
// Section 37 scope limited; the arbitrator found the final bill and the deductions not disputed, yet examined the deductions on merits;
// costs set aside rightly for failure to consider the Section 31A(3) factors (4). No other contention pressed (5). Section 37: only
// whether Section 34 was exercised within its four corners (6; AC Chokshi, (2025) 5 SCC 321; SEPCO, (2026) 2 SCC 542). The Single
// Judge examined award paras 203-206: CRS did not refute the final bill or the deductions; it failed to prove its own bill Ex.CW-1/20
// and claimed on IRCON's final bill; the arbitrator observed there was no pleading challenging the final bill or the deductions; the
// deductions were for defects in the work, accepted by CRS; the arbitrator could not independently examine them and went beyond the
// issue in dispute (7). Konkan Railway "of no avail": the arbitrator, contradicting its finding that there was no pleading challenging
// the final bill or the deductions, considered the deductions on merits "acting beyond the referred dispute" (8). Section 31A(3)
// factors (9); the factors were not gone into; partial success not considered; five out of seven claims "found to be frivolous"; costs
// "contrary to the statutory provisions", "vitiated by patent illegality" (10); Om Vajrakaya not applicable (11). "No case is made out
// for interference in the impugned judgement. The appeal is dismissed." (12).
