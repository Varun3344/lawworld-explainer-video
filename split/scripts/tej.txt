# 4 · WHAT SECTION 17A COVERS
10 | The Court read it closely: approval is needed only where the offence relates to a recommendation made, or a decision taken, in official duty.
10 | A bribe demand is neither: a pure criminal act, unconnected to official functions. Earlier rulings, Santosh Karnani and Anil Daima, agree.
# 5 · THE VERDICT
10 | The pending larger bench reference on Section Seventeen A had no bearing.
