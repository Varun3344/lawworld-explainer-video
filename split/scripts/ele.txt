# 1 · GIFT OR TRUST?
10 | Hi, I am Ravishankar from LawWorld. Can a gifted elephant become a breach of trust case? And can cheating and breach of trust be alleged together, on the same facts?
10 | On fifteenth October, twenty twenty six, the Kerala High Court, through a single bench of Honourable Justice A Badharudeen, answered both questions. || On 15 October 2026, the Kerala High Court, through a single bench of Honourable Justice A. Badharudeen, answered both questions.
# 2 · THE ELEPHANT
10 | The petitioner's case: an elephant gifted to a Mutt kept coming into musth, so in twenty seventeen it was gifted back. The donor then gifted it to him. || The petitioner's case: an elephant gifted to a Math kept coming into musth, so in 2017 it was gifted back. The donor then gifted it to him.
10 | The complainant alleged it was only entrusted to him during musth, and not returned when asked. An F I R was registered for breach of trust and cheating. || The complainant alleged it was only entrusted to him during musth, and not returned when asked. An FIR was registered for breach of trust and cheating.
# 3 · A SECOND PETITION
10 | The complainant objected that an earlier petition to quash it was withdrawn without liberty. The Court said mere technicalities would not stand in the way of relief.
# 4 · WHAT THE COURT HELD
10 | The Court held that cheating and criminal breach of trust are fundamentally contradictory, and cannot co-exist on the exact same set of allegations.
10 | The complainant's counsel then pressed only breach of trust. But Section Four Oh Six needs entrustment by a person having dominion over the property. || The complainant's counsel then pressed only breach of trust. But Section 406 needs entrustment by a person having dominion over the property.
10 | One who has parted with title and possession ceases to have dominion. The complainant disputes the gift deeds, but that is for a civil court to decide.
# 5 · THE RESULT
10 | Finding not even primary material for either offence, the Court allowed the petition. The F I R and all further proceedings stand quashed. || Finding not even primary material for either offence, the Court allowed the petition. The FIR and all further proceedings stand quashed.
