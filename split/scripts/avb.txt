// Held: Section 219U(1) permits seizure in two contingencies; the second (on its return after use for an offence) does not need a
// public place or road; Section 219N(2) bars dumping waste in private places; by the Mischief Rule, "return" covers parking on return
// after the offence; the excavator was parked on the spot after the dumping; the seizure justified (11, 12); Section 219U(2):
// confiscation on reasonable ground to believe the vehicle was used for an offence (13); the use for dumping uncontroverted; Ext.P8

# 5 · WHAT THE COURT HELD
10 | Finally, what did the High Court decide? A vehicle returning after the offence can be seized even off a public road, and parking at the spot counts as return.
10 | The plain paper agreement was not a lease deed. Even if it were, the owner took no reasonable care. The confiscation stood; the petition was dismissed.
